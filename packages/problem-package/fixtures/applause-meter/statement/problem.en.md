The applause meter recorded $N$ readings, one per second: $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_N$.
A performance is judged by its loudest stretch of exactly $K$ consecutive seconds.

What is the largest total of any $K$ consecutive readings?

## Input

The first line contains $N$ and $K$ ($1 \le K \le N \le 2 \cdot 10^5$).
The second line contains $a_1, \ldots, a_N$ ($-10^9 \le a_i \le 10^9$).

## Output

Print the largest sum of $K$ consecutive readings.
